About Us

Swantia-style craftsmanship, Lahore roots.

We are a Pakistani-based manufacturer of surgical instruments, focused on producing high-quality forceps, needle holders, scissors, specula and diagnostic instruments.

Since 2014 we have established one of Pakistan's most trusted surgical instrument brands, supplying hospitals, clinics and distributors with reliable, German-standard instruments at affordable prices.

Every instrument is manufactured in Lahore — the world's surgical capital — using AISI 304 stainless steel, and each piece passes multiple quality checks before dispatch.
