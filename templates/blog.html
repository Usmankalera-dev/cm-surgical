Universal Bandage Scissors for Home Use

Universal bandage scissors are a safe, practical tool for wound care at home. The angled blunt tip lets you cut bandages close to the skin without causing injury. When managing minor injuries at home, having the right tool matters — choose a stainless steel pair with a secure, comfortable grip and store it in your first-aid kit for emergencies.

Bed Pans for Home Use

Home-based patient care often requires practical solutions that respect dignity and safety. A quality bed pan — made of non-reactive, easily sanitised material — supports caregivers and patients alike. Always follow medical guidance and choose the correct size and design for the patient's needs.

Hegar Dilators Home Use

Hegar dilators are precision surgical instruments commonly used by healthcare professionals for dilation procedures. Proper care means cleaning immediately after use, sterilising by autoclave, and inspecting for surface wear. Never use dilators outside a clinical setting — always under professional supervision.
